THE FIELD GUIDE

Read the land

An illustrated, deterministic map foundation for an Ancient Era strategy game. Geography and unimproved tile yields follow a researched Civilization VI: Gathering Storm subset. Art and generation are original.

What is modeled

44 × 26 hexes with east–west adjacency wrapping, climate-based biomes, hills, mountains, coasts, oceans, lakes, connected rivers on hex edges, cliffs, freshwater access, movement costs, defense modifiers, natural appeal, continents, resources, and two validated player starts. Tribal villages and barbarian camps are static map markers.

How yields work

Ordinary tile yield = terrain + hills + feature + revealed resource + supported natural-wonder adjacency. Rivers supply fresh water; they do not directly add yields. All yields are unimproved: no mines, farms, policies, pantheons, citizens, or civilization bonuses are included.

Ancient Era visibility

Turn one hides Horses and Iron, including their yield bonuses. Ancient techs shows resources revealed by Animal Husbandry and Bronze Working. Inspector reveals the same Ancient resource pool for map development. These are inspection presets, not a research simulation. Niter, Coal, Oil, Aluminum, Uranium and archaeological sites are deliberately outside this Ancient-only model.

Deliberate simplifications

This does not reproduce Firaxis’s map scripts or balance guarantees. Resource density, luxury allocation, river drainage, coast shelves, cliff placement, continent borders, start scoring and site placement are our heuristics. Both starts require fresh water and two first-ring land tiles with at least 2 Food and 2 Production before improvements and strategic-resource reveals. At least six freshwater city locations, including the starts, are validated at four-hex minimum spacing. Geological regions use irregular boundaries that guide mountain ranges, with occasional passes and interior peaks. Landmass IDs separately track connected islands. Archipelago builds paired home islands and satellite islands separated by sea corridors. Each accepted pair has at most a 15% difference in usable island area and reachable land, three reachable freshwater city sites per player (including the capital, spaced four hexes apart), nearby turn-one Food and Production within 25%, and legal Horses and Iron within eight hexes on the same reachable island. The nearest usable satellite island is at most two hexes farther for one player than the other, measured shoreline to shoreline; this is not a simulated sailing route. Failed maps are deterministically regenerated. These limits do not guarantee equal luxury types, wonder access, naval routes or competitive balance across all future mechanics. The wonder pool is limited to Crater Lake, Great Barrier Reef, Pantanal and Uluru; two eligible wonders are placed. Volcanic eruptions, flood history, volcanic-soil fertility and disasters are not simulated. No NFP game modes, Honey or Maize, off-map luxuries, cities or units.

Controls & portability

Click to inspect; drag to pan; scroll or +/− to zoom. F fits the map, Y toggles yield pips, R resources and G the grid. Arrow keys move the selected tile. Click the minimap to navigate.
